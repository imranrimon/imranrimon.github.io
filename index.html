About

I'm a PhD researcher in Computer Science, working with Dr. Robin Chataut in the NextGen AI Research Lab at Texas Christian University. Right now my work is on assured autonomy — predictive runtime assurance and mission-aware containment that keep autonomous AI agents safe and within their intended limits while they operate.

Before that, I worked on generative modeling — diffusion and flow-based methods, GANs, GNNs, and transformers — for scientific data, plus anomaly detection for power grids and physiological signals; some of it is published at IEEE venues and more is under review. I also spend a lot of time on the practical side: building ML pipelines in PyTorch and TensorFlow that other people can actually reproduce.
